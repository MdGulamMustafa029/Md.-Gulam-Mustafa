01 / ABOUT

A materials perspective.
A physicist’s curiosity.

My work connects the fundamental properties of materials with their potential in energy, sensing, and electromagnetic applications.

Department of Science and Humanities
Bangladesh Army International University of Science and Technology
Cumilla 3501, Bangladesh

I am a Lecturer in Physics with research interests in advanced functional materials, multiferroic and magnetoelectric systems, ferrites, and functional oxide ceramics.

My research explores how composition, doping, and microstructure influence dielectric, magnetic, electrical, optical, and magnetoelectric behavior. My collaborative work also extends to graphene-based terahertz antennas and machine-learning-assisted performance prediction.

Alongside research, I teach physics, support laboratory activities, and contribute to student research supervision.
